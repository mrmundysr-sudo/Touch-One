# Touch One 1.4.0 — premium pass

Rules, artwork and opponents are unchanged. This release is about how the game
*feels*: timing, typography and sound.

## Timing
* **One game clock.** All animation, opponent "thinking" and sound cues run off a single
  clock that only advances while frames are drawn (and is clamped per frame). The game now
  pauses by itself when the app leaves the screen, a frame hitch slows things down instead
  of skipping them, and sound cannot drift from the picture.
* **Moves are played out, not teleported.** Cards deal from the deck, fly to the pile with a
  landing thud, and effects (+2/+4, skip, reverse, wild) play in order before the turn passes.
  Input is locked while a sequence plays, which removes the double-tap/out-of-order bugs.
* **No more stale timers.** The old handler callbacks were never cancelled, so a callback from
  a finished game could fire into the next one. Everything now lives on one cancellable timeline.
* **Opponents think for 0.8–1.2 s** (was a flat 2.2–3.2 s of dead time).
* **Colour wheel is usable after 0.4 s** (was locked for 2.55 s) and ratchets in time with its spin.
  Tapping outside it now cancels instead of trapping you.
* **Selecting a card responds on touch-down** instead of on release; flick a card upward to play it.
* Hold-to-call-Touch-One now shows a charging ring, fires at exactly 750 ms, and the hint
  "Hold the pile to call Touch One" appears whenever you're down to two cards.
* Back button returns to the title screen. Taps are ignored for 0.75 s on the win/lose screens so a
  stray tap can't skip the celebration.

## Typography and look
* One typeface (Outfit Bold, SIL OFL — licence in `assets/fonts/`) used everywhere. The
  "1 / 2 / 3", "Play again" and "End turn" buttons were low-resolution PNGs with baked-in text in a
  generic font; they are now drawn crisply at runtime.
* Cards are rendered once into cached bitmaps with gradients, gloss, a ringed oval, real skip/reverse
  icons and underlined 6/9. Unplayable cards dim on your turn.
* Backgrounds and speech bubbles are scaled with a quality resampler (the 1500 px bubbles were being
  drawn at ~300 px with no filtering, which aliased) and big scenes are loaded only while visible.
* Haptic ticks on select/play/call; clear hints when you have no legal move (deck/End pulse).

## Sound
Every effect was a 0.06–0.55 s flat-amplitude beep (and `sfx_wheel` / `sfx_touchone` were almost
inaudible, peaking at 0.01 / 0.04). All effects are regenerated by `scripts/make_sounds.py`:
paper-slap + wood-thud card sounds, card slides, marimba/bell musical cues, light room reverb, tuned
levels, no clicks. The character voice clips had up to 326 ms of dead air at the start (so they landed
late); that is trimmed. Voices now play on special moments rather than on every single turn.

Regenerate with: `python3 scripts/make_sounds.py source/app/src/main/res/raw`
(needs numpy + scipy; original voice clips are kept in `scripts/voice_originals/`).
